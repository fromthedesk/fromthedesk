What the Desk is Thinking
Week of June 29, 2026 — institutional perspective. Updated weekly.
Quarter-End & Half-Year Flows — Watch the London Fix High impact
All week  ·  Especially Mon Jun 29 & Tue Jun 30  ·  11:00am ET London Fix  ·  All pairs
Corporate FX, quarter-end, and half-year rebalancing all hit Friday. But don't assume it's over — the major players don't always finish in one day. Sovereign wealth funds and pension funds that need to rebalance portfolios don't care about price. They just need to get done. That flow hits hardest around the London Fix at 11:00am ET. If you're in a position around that time and the market moves against you for no obvious reason, this is why. These aren't traders — they're allocators with mandates. They'll move the market whether it's convenient or not. Watch the fix closely Monday and Tuesday especially.
USDJPY — Watch 161.80/162.00 High impact
All week  ·  JPY
USDJPY is approaching levels that have historically triggered Bank of Japan intervention. The market knows it and traders will be watching every tick. What makes this week particularly interesting is the July 4th US holiday — thin liquidity and a skeleton crew on desks is exactly the environment the BOJ has historically favored to maximize intervention impact. A smaller amount of capital moves the market further when nobody is around to push back. If you're trading JPY pairs this week, know where you are. Intervention risk doesn't announce itself.
⚠ Wednesday July 1st — Busiest Day of the Week
Three major events land on Wednesday: the Sintra central bank panel at 9:00am ET with Warsh, Lagarde, Bailey and Macklem, ISM Manufacturing at 10:00am ET, and JOLTS at 10:00am ET. USD and EUR pairs will be volatile all morning. Have your positions managed before the open. EUR pairs in particular will be volatile all morning. Have your levels defined before the open.
German Unemployment Change Medium impact
Tuesday, June 30  ·  3:55am ET  ·  EUR
Germany's labor market has been deteriorating for two years running and is sitting near six-year highs. The May reading ticked down slightly but the Federal Employment Agency called it a one-off effect and warned that unemployment is expected to rise further. With the usual spring rebound failing to materialize, this week's number is worth watching — a worse-than-expected print alongside soft EU CPI gives the ECB more reason to pause its hiking cycle. Watch EURUSD heading into the Sintra panel later that morning.
EU CPI Flash Estimate High impact
Tuesday, June 30  ·  5:00am ET  ·  EUR
The ECB hiked at their last meeting and inflation is still the focus. A soft print this week won't undo the hike, but it could temper expectations for further moves. Core is what matters more than headline — energy distortions are well understood at this point. A surprise to the upside keeps the hiking cycle intact and gives Lagarde a hawkish platform at Sintra four hours later. The combination of this number and the panel makes Tuesday morning the highest-risk window of the week for EUR pairs.
ECB Sintra Forum — Central Bank Panel High impact
Wednesday, July 1  ·  9:00am ET  ·  Sintra, Portugal  ·  EUR/USD/GBP/CAD
The most important event of the week. Lagarde, Warsh, Bailey, and Macklem all on stage together at the ECB Sintra Forum on the final day of the conference. This isn't a prepared speech — it's a live panel with questions, which means unscripted responses from four of the world's most important central bankers at the same time. Watch Warsh especially — every word will be parsed for rate signals given the hawkish tone from his first FOMC. With EU CPI dropping the same morning, Lagarde's comments on inflation could move EURUSD instantly. Have your positions managed before 9am.
US ISM Manufacturing PMI Medium impact
Wednesday, July 1  ·  10:00am ET  ·  USD
Manufacturing has been on a solid run — May came in at 54, the strongest reading since May 2022. Consensus for June is sitting at 53.7 so the bar is set high and the market is expecting continued expansion. A miss below consensus raises questions about whether the momentum is fading. A beat keeps the growth narrative intact and gives Warsh more reason to stay hawkish. Watch prices paid — pressure has remained elevated and a hot reading alongside strong activity gives the Fed exactly the cover it needs to hold rates higher for longer.
US JOLTS Job Openings High impact
Wednesday, July 1  ·  10:00am ET  ·  USD
The labor market is the Fed's other mandate and right now it's under a microscope. With the next FOMC on July 28-29 and the market still digesting Warsh's hawkish tone, every labor data point matters more than usual. A soft JOLTS number reinforces the case for rate cuts and softens USD. A strong number keeps the higher-for-longer trade alive. Watch the quits rate inside the report — that's the real signal on labor market health.
US Non-Farm Payrolls High impact
Thursday, July 2  ·  8:30am ET  ·  USD
Consensus is sitting at 114k. Coming off a week with JOLTS and the Sintra panel, the market will be primed for this. A number below 100k changes the conversation entirely and brings rate cuts back onto the table fast. A number above 150k keeps Warsh's hawkish stance looking justified. The first move after the print will be violent — let the dust settle before you do anything. Note this drops Thursday July 2nd — US markets observe the July 4th holiday on Friday July 3rd. Expect an early close and thin conditions after the number hits.
US Independence Day — Market Holiday Medium impact
Friday, July 3 (observed)  ·  All Day  ·  USD
July 4th falls on a Saturday this year so the US is observing the holiday on Friday July 3rd — the same day as NFP. US markets close early after the jobs report. FX is still open but volume drops off fast after the number hits. Expect thin conditions from Thursday afternoon through the weekend. The BOJ has historically loved this kind of low-liquidity environment if they decide to intervene in JPY. Know your risk going into the long weekend.
